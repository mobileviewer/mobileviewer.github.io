Guide

How the Battery Test Works

01

Detect Battery API

The tool checks if your browser supports the navigator.getBattery() API. Most Chromium‑based browsers on desktop and Android provide this.

02

Read Battery Metrics

It reads battery level (0–1), charging status, charging time, discharging time, and — when available — voltage and temperature.

03

Display & Monitor

All values are displayed in real time. Click Live Monitor to subscribe to battery events and see a timestamped log of changes.

💡
Pro Tip: Use the Live Monitor while charging to see how quickly your battery fills up. This can help you identify fast‑charging behaviour or potential battery issues.
